WEAPONS
-------
weapons.glb is a separate Quaternius pack (CC0). Its OWN rig is incompatible
(43 bones) and is discarded — only the gun meshes are used: AK, SMG, Sniper,
Sniper_2, Shotgun, RocketLauncher, Pistol, Revolver, ShortCannon, Shovel,
GrenadeLauncher. See "Getting a foreign weapon mesh into the hand" in
SPRITE_PIPELINE.md for the three traps involved.

SCENERY
-------
art/props/*.glb — Quaternius huts, palms and sandbags (CC0), rendered to 2D by
tools/render_props.py through the same camera as the soldiers. Provenance is the
poly.pizza CDN UUID in each filename.

WHY THESE FIVE
--------------
Every model in the pack shares ONE 62-bone skeleton and the SAME 24 mocap clips
(Idle_Gun, Idle_Gun_Pointing, Run_Shoot, Death, HitRecieve...). They are therefore
drop-in interchangeable in tools/render_model_sprites.py, while being visibly
different people. That is what makes per-faction bodies possible at no cost to the
animation pipeline.

Most of them carry separate head meshes with Skin, Eye, Eyebrows and Hair
materials — they have faces. The swat is fully masked and gloved, which is why
every soldier used to read as the same anonymous mannequin.

CORRECTION (2026-08-18): this used to say "all of them EXCEPT swat". That is
wrong. Read straight out of the GLB material lists, `soldier` has no Eye or
Eyebrows either — its materials are Black, Swat, Skin, Grey, Hair_Brown, Brown.
So rifleman and sniper are as faceless as the engineer. It does not matter at
84px on the field, and it is exactly why the HUD-portrait experiment was
rejected (see PLAN.md issue queue item 3). Faces present: casual, farmer,
worker, adventurer, hoodie. Faces absent: soldier, swat.

WHICH UNIT USES WHICH  (see MODEL_FOR in tools/render_model_sprites.py)
  casual      -> rifleman, arvn, m60, engineer   (plain shirt + trousers; webbing added)
  adventurer  -> recon, sniper                   (own backpack mesh, taller silhouette)
  worker      -> nva, rpd
  farmer      -> guerrilla, sapper, marksman     (peasant clothing; right for the VC)
  swat        -> unused, kept as a fallback donor

Hair and moustache faces are deleted for any unit wearing headgear (strip_hair) —
these are civilian models and several ship a ponytail.
